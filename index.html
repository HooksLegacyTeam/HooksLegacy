The Story

Uncover the Secrets of the Doomed Cabin

You awaken in the captain's cabin of an ancient pirate ship. Before you sits a mysterious box, covered in cryptic symbols and locked with mechanisms unknown to modern men.

Open it. Examine the coins, the ancient hook, the globe that seems to move on its own. Every corner hides a clue. Every puzzle brings you closer to the truth — and closer to the Kraken's fury.

Do you have what it takes to solve the final puzzle and survive?
