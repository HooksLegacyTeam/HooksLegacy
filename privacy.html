1. Data Controller

Hooks Legacy Development

2. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • This website (wearekeepersofthesea.com) — a landing page for the game Hook's Legacy.
  • The mobile game "We are Keepers of The Sea: Hook's Legacy — Episode 1" available on Google Play.

Both the website and the game are designed to collect minimal data. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

3. Data Collected by This Website

This website operates on a privacy-by-design basis and collects no personal data.

Cookies

The website uses no cookies of any kind — no tracking cookies, no analytics cookies, no third-party cookies.

Analytics and Tracking

The website uses no analytics tools — no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no tracking scripts of any kind.

Forms and Contact

The website contains no forms for contact, registration, login, or data submission. No email addresses, names, or personal information are collected through this website.

localStorage

The website may use localStorage solely to remember your language preference (if applicable). This data remains entirely on your device and is never transmitted to any server.

Google Fonts

This website uses Google Fonts to display typography. When you visit the site, your IP address may be sent to Google's servers to deliver the fonts. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Collected by the Game

The mobile game We are Keepers of The Sea: Hook's Legacy — Episode 1 may collect certain information automatically:

Automatically Collected Information

  • Type of mobile device
  • Device unique ID and IP address
  • Operating system and version
  • Interactions within the game

Advertising (AdMob)

The game integrates Google AdMob for advertisements. AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID)
  • Interaction data related to ads shown in the game

AdMob uses this data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

For the website: processing is based on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for serving a static landing page with no data collection.

For the game: processing is based on consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for AdMob data collection, and legitimate interest for basic game functionality data.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • Data collected through AdMob may be shared with Google for ad delivery and analytics purposes.
  • Data may be disclosed to comply with legal requirements or protect our rights and safety.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights (GDPR)

In accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR — Reg. EU 2016/679), you have the right to:

  • Access (Art. 15) — Know if we process your data and obtain a copy.
  • Rectification (Art. 16) — Correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure (Art. 17) — Request deletion of your data.
  • Restriction (Art. 18) — Restrict processing.
  • Portability (Art. 20) — Receive your data in a structured format.
  • Objection (Art. 21) — Object to processing.
  • Complaint (Art. 77) — File a complaint with the relevant supervisory authority.

9. Data Retention

The website retains no personal data. Game data collected by AdMob is retained according to Google's data retention policies.

10. International Data Transfers

Google Fonts and AdMob may involve the transfer of data to servers located outside the European Economic Area. Google has adhered to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Adequacy Decision of July 10, 2023).

11. Children's Privacy

Both the website and the game are not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that such information has been collected, it will be promptly deleted.
